About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Software Engineering at the School of Artificial Intelligence and Computer Science, Jiangnan University, Wuxi, China, working in the multimodal group of Prof. Xiao-Jun Wu and Prof. Josef Kittler. From 2025 to 2026 I am a CSC-funded visiting Ph.D. student at the Centre for Vision, Speech and Signal Processing (CVSSP), University of Surrey, United Kingdom.

My research centres on multimodal image fusion with an emphasis on robustness in complex environments — in particular, addressing the severe performance degradation that existing fusion methods suffer under adverse weather conditions. My work spans low-level vision tasks such as image registration, fusion and super-resolution, and the efficient deployment of models on edge devices. I also work with large language models, including fine-tuning and application deployment.
